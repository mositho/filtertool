# Path of Exile filter terminology

- **Area Level** — the level of the area where an item drops, not the character's or item's level.
- **Min Area Level** — the lowest Area Level where a selection applies.
- **Max Area Level** — the highest Area Level where a selection applies. No Max Area Level means no area-level upper bound.
- **Item Level** — the level of an individual item. It can differ from the Area Level.
- **Min Item Level** — the lowest Item Level a selection matches.
- **Max Item Level** — the highest Item Level a selection matches.
- **Drop Level** — the minimum level at which an item base can drop.
- **Item Class** — a category of items, such as One Hand Axes or Life Flasks.
- **Base Type** — a particular item base within an Item Class, such as Rusted Hatchet.
- **Weapon phase** — a selection of weapon classes or bases with optional Min and Max Area Levels. Phases can overlap; the more-specific range has priority for matching weapons.
- **Highlight** — a selection of items given distinctive presentation, such as a style, icon or sound.
